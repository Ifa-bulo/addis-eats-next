# Addis Eats image checklist

## Included assets

- `public/images/hero/addis-eats-delivery.webp` is the supplied hero image, showing an Ethiopian woman receiving a delivery. It is optimized to approximately 66 KB.
- `public/og-image.jpg` is the 1200 × 630 social-card image, approximately 112 KB.
- Lightweight SVG dish illustrations are included so every menu card has a visible local asset before you upload real food photography.

For menu photos, a 4:3 landscape crop works well. Compress each image to about 80–145 KB, use consistent lighting, and keep the dish centered. Avoid huge camera originals; the goal is a quick first visit on mobile. Keep meaningful alt text in the card component if an image becomes dish-specific.
